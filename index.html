Frequently Asked Questions

Is Little Genius Kids really free and ad-free?

Yes. All games, age dashboards, subject lessons, and printable worksheets currently on Little Genius Kids are free to access and ad-free.

Do children need an account or personal information to use the site?

No. Children can play games, complete lessons, and print worksheets without registering. Progress stars and learning preferences stay locally in your browser's storage.

How should parents use the age levels (2–3, 3–5, 5–7, 8–12)?

Age levels are helpful starting suggestions, not strict rules. Every child grows differently, so feel free to explore easier or more advanced subjects anytime.
