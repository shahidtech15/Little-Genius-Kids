About Math Puzzle Game — Learning Guide

Ages 3–7 Mathematics Beginner • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Practice single-digit and early two-digit addition facts with clear visuals and immediate, encouraging step-by-step feedback.

  • Connecting spoken number words smoothly to written horizontal addition equations.
  • Using the count-on mental strategy (starting from the larger number and counting up).
  • Typing two-digit numeric answers accurately using keyboard or touch keypad inputs.

How to Play & Use

Solve the addition problem displayed inside the bright puzzle box, type your numeric answer into the field, and press Enter or click Submit Answer. Click Next Puzzle for a new pair of numbers.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Whenever a child pauses on sums above 10, place 10 small blocks, buttons, or spoons nearby so they can count the combined total physically before typing. Praise careful checking just as much as quick answers.

Offline Extension: Take 10 LEGO bricks or dried beans, hide a few under a cup, and let your child count the visible ones to deduce how many hidden pieces remain inside the cup.
