About AI Slide Puzzle — Learning Guide

Ages 5–12 Logic & AI Intermediate • 7 mins

Learning Objectives

Develop spatial planning, step-by-step algorithmic thinking, and tile-ordering logic used in classic computer science search algorithms.

  • Sequencing multi-step tile moves before acting rather than guessing randomly.
  • Understanding state spaces and goal states — core ideas behind puzzle-solving AI.
  • Building patience, working memory, and visual pattern tracking across a 3x3 sliding grid.

How to Play & Use

Click any tile adjacent to the empty slot to slide it into the open space. Work row by row starting with tiles 1, 2, and 3 across the top row, then align tiles 4 through 8 in ascending order to activate the robot logic core.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Ask your child which tile they want to position two turns ahead. Explaining a strategy aloud turns simple sliding play into genuine algorithmic reasoning.

Offline Extension: Create a physical 3x3 grid on paper and slide eight numbered index cards or bottle caps around one open space so kids experience look-ahead planning away from screens.
