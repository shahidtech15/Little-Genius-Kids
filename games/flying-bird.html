About Flying Bird — Learning Guide

Ages 5–12 Logic & Coordination Intermediate • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Sharpen timing, rhythm anticipation, and hand-eye coordination through controlled single-tap obstacle navigation.

  • Predicting vertical arcs and downward gravity timing well before approaching narrow gaps.
  • Maintaining calm focus, steady impulse control, and consistent reaction cadence across obstacles.
  • Self-monitoring personal best scores while pacing short, frustration-free play breaks.

How to Play & Use

Click the game canvas, tap the screen, or press the Spacebar to give the bird a gentle upward wing flap. Thread cleanly through the open gaps between pipes while steering clear of top and bottom edges.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Encourage short two-minute rounds and talk about why steady, gentle taps work better than rapid panicky clicks — a helpful lesson in impulse control and rhythm.

Offline Extension: Practice steady rhythm offline by clapping or bouncing a soft balloon in the air with gentle upward taps, counting each controlled tap up to twenty.
