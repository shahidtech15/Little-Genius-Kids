About Pattern Recognition — Learning Guide

Ages 3–8 Logical Thinking Beginner • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Teach visual rule discovery and sequence prediction — a core skill behind early algebra, music rhythm, and machine learning.

  • Identifying repeating ABAB, AABB, ABC, and alternating growth sequences cleanly.
  • Checking the full row from left to right to eliminate look-alike distractors.
  • Explaining the repeating rule aloud before selecting the next missing symbol.

How to Play & Use

Examine the sequence of playful icons across each round, predict which symbol logically continues the pattern next, and click your matching choice. Finish all 8 progressive rounds to complete the set.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Ask your child to chant the pattern like a beat ("star, moon, star, moon — what comes next?") before tapping. Hearing the rhythm makes abstract sequence rules click fast for early learners.

Offline Extension: Line up spoons and forks or two colors of LEGO bricks on the kitchen table in an AAB pattern and ask your child to extend the next three pieces aloud.
