About Star Catcher — Learning Guide

Ages 3–7 Coordination & Counting Beginner • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Build gentle pointer tracking, visual focus, and playful early counting skills designed for preschool and early primary learners.

  • Sliding a collector smoothly across the screen in sync with drifting star targets.
  • Filtering shiny target stars from obstacles before moving into position.
  • Practicing counting aloud by ones and fives as score totals increase.

How to Play & Use

Slide your catcher left and right across the starry sky to scoop up falling stars before they pass the bottom edge. Dodge hazards and see how high your star count climbs.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

An approachable starter game for kids still learning mouse dragging or touch tracking! Count caught stars aloud together up to 10 or 20 for built-in math practice.

Offline Extension: Cut 10 yellow paper stars, scatter them around the room, and time a playful scavenger walk counting each star back into a basket.
