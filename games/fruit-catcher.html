About Fruit Catcher — Learning Guide

Ages 3–7 Logic & Motor Skills Beginner • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Practice visual tracking, left-to-right directional control, and quick sorting while recognizing healthy fruits.

  • Tracking multiple falling items simultaneously and choosing whichever target lands first.
  • Coordinating arrow keys, mouse pointer, or touch drags smoothly with horizontal basket position.
  • Naming healthy fruits aloud and reinforcing skip-counting habits as scores climb.

How to Play & Use

Slide your basket smoothly left and right along the bottom of the screen to catch falling fruits and earn points. Watch closely as drops gradually speed up and steer clear of non-fruit hazards.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

With younger kids, name each fruit and its color out loud as they catch it, or count together by ones and twos to weave numeracy naturally into play.

Offline Extension: Sort real apples, bananas, or toy kitchen items into two bowls at home by color or size, then count how many pieces landed in each bowl.
