About Reaction Ninja — Learning Guide

Ages 5–12 Focus & Reflex Intermediate • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Improve selective visual attention, peripheral scanning speed, and accurate mouse or touch targeting while filtering distractions.

  • Spotting and clicking pop-up targets quickly inside a wide play field.
  • Distinguishing valid targets from decoys or empty space to keep accuracy high.
  • Practicing calm, balanced eye scanning instead of tense erratic clicking.

How to Play & Use

Press Start to launch the game timer and watch closely as active colored targets appear across the play zone. Click or tap valid targets swiftly before they vanish while avoiding wasted misclicks.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Compare speed against accuracy together after a round: rushing blindly creates missed taps, whereas calm peripheral scanning builds steady hand control and reliable precision across interactive games, early reading, and keyboard typing.

Offline Extension: Toss a soft beanbag or rolled sock gently from left to right hand while calling out a target color to catch, practicing calm focus, visual tracking, and quick hand readiness.
