Topic 1 • Spoken Sounds & Recognition

ABCs, Numbers 1–10 & Everyday Objects

Learning Objectives: Recognize first letters, count objects 1 to 10 aloud, and name everyday items.

Sit beside your toddler and tap one card at a time. Repeat the spoken word together and connect it to something in the room (e.g., "A is for Apple", "2 shoes").

Topic 2 • Visual World

Colors, Shapes, Animals & Fruits

Toddlers learn fastest by comparing bright colors, basic shapes (circle, square, triangle), friendly animals, and familiar fruits & vegetables.

Topic 3 • Music & Screen-Free Play

Nursery Rhymes & Parent-Guided Activities

  • Sound & Clap: Sing a nursery rhyme slowly while clapping on each beat.
  • Sort by Color: Put 4 red blocks and 4 blue blocks into two bowls together.
  • Point & Name: Play "Where is your nose? Where are your ears?" for body-parts vocabulary.
