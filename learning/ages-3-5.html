Uppercase & Lowercase Letters, Phonics & Tracing

Learning Objectives: Connect letter shapes (Aa, Bb, Cc) to their starting sounds and build pencil control through tracing.

School Readiness

Days, Months, Weather, Healthy Habits & Manners

  • Calendar & Weather: Name today's day of the week and check if the sky is sunny, cloudy, or rainy.
  • Healthy Habits: Wash hands before meals, brush teeth morning and night, and drink clean water.
  • Good Manners: Practice saying "Please", "Thank you", and taking turns during games.

About Preschool Daily Routine Tip — Learning Guide

Ages 3–5 School Readiness Beginner • 15 mins

Learning Objectives

Build joyful kindergarten readiness through short phonics, counting, sorting, and good-manners routines.

  • Combine 5 minutes of interactive letter/number tracing with tactile playdough or crayon drawing.
  • Narrate weather, days of the week, and simple patterns during breakfast or walks.
  • Keep praise focused on effort rather than memorizing everything on the first try.

How to Play & Use

Pick one Literacy card and one Numbers or Manners card each day, then wrap up with the quick 3-question mini quiz above.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

For 3–5-year-olds, finishing while they still feel excited for one more turn builds lasting enthusiasm for tomorrow.

Offline Extension: Practice zipping jackets, pouring water into cups, and naming three colors in a picture book to pair life skills with early academics.
