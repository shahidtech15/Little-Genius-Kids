About Parent & Teacher Dashboard — Learning Guide

Adults & Educators Progress & Controls Family Guide • 5 mins

Learning Objectives

Review locally stored learning stars, study time, sound preferences, and healthy daily screen limits privately inside your browser.

  • Monitoring earned stars and active study time locally without creating remote child accounts.
  • Customizing spoken audio effects, dark mode, or high-contrast display for learner comfort.
  • Configuring practical daily screen-time reminders to balance online play with offline activities.

How to Play & Use

Inspect your device’s locally stored progress cards above, adjust audio or daily time-limit preferences using the controls provided, or reset local stars anytime when sharing a device across learners.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Little Genius Kids keeps zero personal child profiles on remote servers — every preference and star counter stays privately on your local browser.

Offline Extension: Sit together once a week to review earned stars, celebrate favorite subjects, and pick one printed worksheet or hands-on kitchen experiment for the weekend.
