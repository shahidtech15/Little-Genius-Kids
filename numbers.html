About Numbers 1–100 & Counting — Learning Guide

Ages 2–7 Mathematics Beginner • 10 mins

Learning Objectives

Develop number recognition, one-to-one quantity sense, and skip-counting fluency from 1 all the way through 100.

  • Connecting spoken number words cleanly to written single- and two-digit numerals.
  • Visualizing quantity with dot groups so children grasp what numbers actually represent.
  • Spotting tens and ones column patterns across rows of the hundred chart.

How to Play & Use

Click any bright tile on the 1–100 grid to hear the number spoken aloud and see its visual quantity representation. Start with 1–10 for toddlers, then explore skip-counting patterns by 2s, 5s, and 10s.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Treat the chart like an exploration map: pick one vertical column (such as 4, 14, 24, 34) and ask your child what ending digit every number in that column shares.

Offline Extension: Count real stairs, fruit slices, or wooden blocks up to 20 together, grouping items neatly into bundles of tens and ones so written digits match physical objects.
