Children's Privacy Policy

Little Genius Kids is designed for children aged 2–12 under parent or teacher supervision. Protecting young learners' privacy is a core engineering rule of this platform.

1. Zero Child Accounts or Personal Collection

  • We do not require children to create accounts, log in, or submit full names, birth dates, home addresses, school names, phone numbers, or photos.
  • There are no public child profiles, public chatrooms, or child-to-child messaging features.
  • Internal search queries on search.html run locally in your browser against data/catalog.json and are not sent to external tracking services.

2. Local-First Browser Storage

Progress stars, active learning time, completed lesson checklists, theme/contrast preferences, and optional local parent profile names are stored only on your device using browser localStorage. Parents can clear all saved progress anytime from the Parent Dashboard ("Reset Progress") or by clearing browser site data.

3. Ad-Free & No Behavioral Tracking

Child learning pages contain zero third-party behavioral advertising and zero third-party analytics trackers by default. External video links on Rhymes & Videos are clearly labeled for parent oversight.

4. Parent & Guardian Requests

If you believe any personal information was submitted through our adult contact channels, email support@little-genius-kids.vercel.app and we will review and delete it promptly.
