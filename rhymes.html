About Nursery Rhymes & Sing-Along — Learning Guide

Ages 2–6 English & Rhythm Beginner • 10 mins

Learning Objectives

Support auditory memory, rhythm, and phonological rhyming awareness through classic nursery rhymes and movement.

  • Hearing matching ending word sounds (cat/hat, star/are) — a key pre-reading skill.
  • Expanding spoken vocabulary and natural sentence cadence through joyful repetition.
  • Developing coordination and listening attention through simple accompanying hand gestures.

How to Play & Use

Browse the cheerful rhyme cards above, start a sing-along verse together, and follow the musical rhythm, lyrics, and hand actions aloud. Replay favorite verses anytime to build memory and verbal confidence.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Pause playfully right before the rhyming word at the end of a familiar line ("Twinkle, twinkle, little ___") so your child joyfully fills in the missing rhyming word.

Offline Extension: Clap along steadily to the rhythm of any favorite nursery rhyme during car rides or cleanup time and invent silly new rhyming words for toys around the room.
