Lesson 1 • Ages 4–8 • Biology

Living Things, Plants & the Water Cycle

Learning Objectives: Distinguish living from nonliving things and trace the water cycle (evaporation, condensation, precipitation).

Living things grow, need food or water, breathe, and reproduce. Green plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make their own food via photosynthesis. Meanwhile, Earth recycles water continuously as the Sun warms oceans (evaporation), water vapor forms clouds (condensation), and rain or snow falls back down (precipitation).

Lesson 2 • Ages 6–12 • Chemistry & Physics

States of Matter, Elements & Energy

  • Solid: Keeps its own shape and volume (ice, wood, rock).
  • Liquid: Flows to take its container's shape (water, milk).
  • Gas: Spreads out to fill available space (air, steam).

Lesson 3 • Ages 6–12 • Astronomy

The Solar System & Gravity

Eight planets orbit the Sun, held in place by gravity: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Earth takes 24 hours to spin once (creating day and night) and ~365 days to orbit the Sun.
