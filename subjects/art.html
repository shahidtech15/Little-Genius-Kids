About At-Home & Classroom Art Extension — Learning Guide

Ages 2–12 Art & Creativity Beginner • 15 mins

Learning Objectives

Blend digital color-mixing exploration with hands-on drawing, paper collage, and imaginative storytelling.

  • Mix primary red, yellow, and blue paint or crayons on paper to verify secondary colors.
  • Spot geometric shapes inside buildings, trees, and animals before sketching outlines.
  • Use open-ended prompts to encourage original student compositions.

How to Play & Use

Read the color and shape mini-lessons above, complete the 3-question quiz, and jump straight into Digital Coloring Canvas or Emoji Art Studio.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Ask open questions about finished drawings ("Tell me about this part!") instead of guessing what an abstract shape represents.

Offline Extension: Cut recycled cardboard or colorful paper scraps into circles, triangles, and squares to assemble a tactile robot or rocket collage.
