About Bilingual Urdu Practice Tips — Learning Guide

Ages 2–12 Urdu Language Beginner • 15 mins

Learning Objectives

Support right-to-left Urdu letter recognition, clear spoken pronunciation, and everyday family vocabulary.

  • Trace right-to-left strokes starting from Alif (ا) and the Bay (ب) family dot placements.
  • Distinguish related letters by dot count and position above or below the curve.
  • Speak greeting phrases, colors, and Urdu numbers aloud naturally at home.

How to Play & Use

Browse the Huroof-e-Tahajji cards and counting guide above, complete the bilingual quiz, and toggle site language settings anytime.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Grouping letters with shared base shapes (ب، پ، ت، ٹ، ث) helps young beginners master dot differences twice as fast.

Offline Extension: Label five familiar kitchen or bedroom items with bilingual English and Urdu sticky notes to reinforce everyday reading.
