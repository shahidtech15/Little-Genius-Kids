Lesson 1 • Ages 3–7 • Beginner

Counting, Addition & Subtraction

Learning Objectives: Combine quantities with addition (+) and find what remains with subtraction (−).

Addition puts groups together (e.g., 7 + 5 = 12). Subtraction takes away or compares two numbers (e.g., 14 − 6 = 8). Counting forward on a number line helps check addition, while counting back helps check subtraction.

Lesson 2 • Ages 6–10 • Intermediate

Multiplication & Division

Learning Objectives: Understand multiplication as equal groups and division as fair sharing.
Equal Groups Example: 4 baskets with 3 apples each = 4 × 3 = 12 apples total. Sharing 12 apples fairly among 4 friends gives 12 ÷ 4 = 3 apples each!

Lesson 3 • Ages 7–12 • Intermediate

Fractions, Decimals, Time & Geometry

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole. The bottom number (denominator) tells how many equal parts make the whole; the top number (numerator) tells how many parts are counted. For example, 1/2 = 0.50, and 3/4 = 0.75.
