Lesson 1 • Ages 3–5 • Beginner

Phonics, Vowels & CVC Blending

Learning Objectives: Distinguish the 5 vowels (A, E, I, O, U) from consonants and blend 3-letter CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) words.

Every English word needs at least one vowel sound. When you say each letter sound slowly and slide them together, you can read hundreds of short words!

Lesson 2 • Ages 5–7 • Intermediate

Nouns, Verbs & Adjectives

Learning Objectives: Identify naming words (nouns), action words (verbs), and describing words (adjectives) inside a sentence.
  • Noun: Names a person, place, animal, or thing (teacher, park, rabbit, book).
  • Verb: Shows an action or state (run, read, write, laugh).
  • Adjective: Describes a noun by color, size, or feeling (tall, blue, happy, warm).
Example Sentence: "The happy (adjective) puppy (noun) runs (verb) in the park."

Lesson 3 • Ages 7–12 • Intermediate

Sentence Construction, Tenses & Punctuation

Use past tense for actions already finished (walked), present tense for now (walks), and future tense for what will happen (will walk). End statements with a period (.), questions with (?), and excited sentences with (!).
