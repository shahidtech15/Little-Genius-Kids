About Colors & Shapes Explorer — Learning Guide

Ages 2–5 Art & Early Math Beginner • 8 mins

Learning Objectives

Introduce essential colors and basic 2D geometric shapes with crisp visuals and clear spoken audio pronunciation.

  • Naming primary and secondary colors accurately across daily objects and clothing.
  • Distinguishing circles, squares, triangles, rectangles, ovals, and stars by sides and corners.
  • Grouping toys and classroom supplies cleanly by color or shape category.

How to Play & Use

Tap any bright color swatch or geometric shape card on screen to highlight it cleanly and listen to its name pronounced aloud. Practice saying each name together and trace corners and edges slowly in the air.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Connecting digital cards to everyday physical objects turns a quick two-minute screen activity into lasting early math vocabulary. Ask why a rolling bicycle wheel needs to be a circle rather than a square!

Offline Extension: Go on a fun 3-minute household scavenger hunt: ask your child to bring back one bright blue toy and one rectangular book from around the room to match what they tapped on screen.
